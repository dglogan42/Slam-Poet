# Loud Enough

A contemporary YA novel.

## Contents

- [`outline.md`](outline.md) — story bible: premise, characters, setting, themes, chapter map
- [`chapters/`](chapters) — the manuscript, one file per chapter (14 chapters)

## Logline

Sixteen-year-old Bea Santos keeps her family's diner running, translates for her grandmother, and never asks for anything for herself — until a statewide poetry slam final lands on the same weekend as the diner's biggest night of the year, and she has to find out whether she's allowed to want something that isn't for someone else.

## Reading order

Start with `chapters/01-closing-shift.md` and read in numeric order through `chapters/14-open-mic.md`.
